Account & Data Deletion Request

Librixa Audiobook Player • Developer: Amr Emam

At Librixa, we prioritize your data ownership and privacy rights. As a Librixa user, you have full control over your account and synced information. You can request the complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

1 Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account and clear all local & cloud synced data instantly inside the Librixa mobile app:

  1. Open Librixa on your Android phone or Wear OS watch.
  2. Navigate to Settings (الإعدادات) > Account & Sync (الحساب والمزامنة).
  3. Tap "Delete Account & Clear Data".
  4. Confirm your request. All synced listening progress, bookmarks, and account metadata will be purged immediately.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and data protection regulations (GDPR/CCPA), here is the exact breakdown of data handling upon deletion:

Data Category Deletion Status Retention Period & Details
Account Credentials & Profile Permanently Deleted Purged immediately from cloud servers within 24-72 hours.
Synced Playback Progress & Timestamps Permanently Deleted All position markers, chapter progress, and playback stats removed permanently.
Bookmarks, Notes & Favorites Permanently Deleted All user-created audio bookmarks, favorite tags, and categories erased completely.
Google Drive Authorization Tokens Revoked & Erased OAuth tokens are revoked and cleared from database. You can also revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
Downloaded Audio Files (Local Device) User Controlled (Device Storage) Audio files stored on your Android device storage remain under your local control until deleted via the app or file manager.
Security & Abuse Prevention Logs Temporary Retention (Max 30 Days) Anonymous request timestamps retained for up to 30 days solely to prevent fraud and satisfy legal compliance obligations, then automatically purged.
